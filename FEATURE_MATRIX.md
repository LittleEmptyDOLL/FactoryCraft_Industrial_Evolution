# Feature matrix — 0.25.1

0.25.1 corrects solid fields to full fixed-center irregular ellipsoids and replaces the invented furnace with Stone/Steel/Electric (fuel ×1 / fuel ×2 / FE ×2). Fuel containers, fractional work/FE, two electric module slots and current-format process persistence are covered by targeted native tests. See FURNACES.md and RESOURCE_FIELDS.md. Previous 0.25.0 implements native 1:10 fluid quantities/capacities, exact percentage-yield oil points and paid pumpjack cycles, deterministic configurable surface oil fields, finite irregular 3D solid bodies, a searchable stable-ID recipe selector and actual vanilla smelting in furnaces only. Old FactoryCraft formats are intentionally unsupported. Pumpjack speed/efficiency/beacons work; productivity extraction remains unsupported and its modules are rejected. Existing four-input machines/four-pack labs do not gain unrelated tiers; extended recipe schemas and browser metadata are bounded. Current checks, costs and remaining CI are in VALIDATION.md / BENCHMARKS.md.


0.24.1 optimizes existing fluids: no connectivity rebuild on content notifications, local true-leaf edits, indexed pending work, touched-build cancellation, budgeted ordering/publication preparation and cached chunk/update deduplication. Rates/recipes/schema stay 0.24.0. A real 10000-pipe server profile/restart and 100/1000/5000/10000 kernel profiles are included; improvements and tail regressions are separated in BENCHMARKS.md.

0.24.0 replaces runtime passive equalization with shared component-sensitive pipe/tank segments, exact checkpoint-share splits, 320×320 extent, physical pressure quotas, powered directional pumps and segment UI/circuit reads. Recipes/FE/assembly-4 ports and required finite chains remain. See FLUIDS.md and VALIDATION.md.

0.23.0 adds six-type inserter reflection, exclusive physical lane/input deposition, the late capacity prerequisite, original tier belt/terminal/splitter/lift hardware and cell-local bounded geometry. Atlas animation remains global when circuits stop a belt. See BELTS.md, INSERTERS.md and VALIDATION.md.

0.22.0 adds typed multi-signal direct four-color circuits, independent I/O masks, structured Constant/Arithmetic/Decider/Selector/Time, indexed pipeline readings and guarded machine/team-research control. The 0.21.0 inserters/stacks and restored finite integration scenarios remain required. Quality/rocket-weight selector modes remain unavailable. See CIRCUITS.md and VALIDATION.md.

0.20.0 added four full-size one-item poles, explicit persisted copper edges, manual/automatic wiring, whole-foundation square coverage, visible sagging wires, coverage overlays and pole status. Canonical names and optional Jade inspection now work through controllers and parts. See ELECTRICITY.md and VALIDATION.md for exact adaptations/evidence. Schema-4 ports and existing production kernels remain unchanged; old intermediate pole worlds require a fresh fixture.

**Implemented** refers only to the explicit scope in the row. **Partial** is not the entire requested subsystem. Evidence: VALIDATION.md. This release delivers stage 1 and a playable part of stage 2; stages 3–8 are not complete. Previous version 0.18.0 added blue/white independent wire colors and cached whole-splitter motor gates, preserving the existing transport kernel and legacy defaults. Stage 3 and later systems remain partial.
