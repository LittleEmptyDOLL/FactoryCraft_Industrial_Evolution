## 0.31.1 placement and interaction

Select an existing endpoint, then aim at supported ground. The destination tangent follows **camera yaw**, including while looking straight down at the same cell; pitch is irrelevant. Heading families retain actual1:2 half diagonals, not uniform22.5° bins. Existing rail targets use their real tangent. R selects an explicit override (after15 returns to camera); X reverses the start; C/sneak cancels; success clears selection. Server reach, selected slot/anchor UUID, loadedness, permission, inventory and geometry checks remain authoritative.

The local5×5 checker overlay uses green(valid), red(rejected) and amber(pending). A `EndpointHints` key includes start, target/look heading, override, anchor and nearby world revision; at most one new expensive candidate is inspected per game tick. Completed results are cached, with a four-second refresh fallback. It sends no per-frame requests and forces no chunks. A held rail alone does not scan a whole network; MISS/air has no overlay; an active planner retains its bounded64m advisory ray and shows an out-of-reach hover red. Actual placement always requires ordinary reach. ClientLevel.hasChunk is always true in1.21.1, so the actual non-creating client cache probe determines known availability. A temporary client-only unload retains selection; the server validates the anchor UUID and genuine availability. Claims unknown to the client can still reject an advisory green preview.

Physical bed, future vehicle clearance and supports are separate. Empty collision shapes outside the bed do not obstruct trains. Bed replacement accepts normal replaceability plus the `factorycraft:rail_replaceable` tag (flowers/torches/vines/dead bush/lichen), only with empty collision or eligible snow and no fluid/BlockEntity. Glass, containers, machines and world liquids remain protected obstacles; waterlogging is not made passable. Solid berry bushes or other nonreplaceable crops are not silently harvested.

All touched true clearance cells participate in permission snapshots. Stage with neighbor side effects suppressed, post NeoForge multi-place protection, revalidate/debit/publish, then issue deferred loot through NeoForge BlockDropsEvent and neighbor updates. Loot is computed against pre-removal neighbors so double plants drop once; cancellation restores exact blocks and emits no drops. Adjacent collision origins are queried to catch tall fences/pistons; their actual subshapes, rather than whole enclosing voxels, decide obstruction.

Future vehicle hulls follow the same two-bogie pose as live stock, sampled at≤.125m and bounded by continuous translation plus measured corner-rotation expansion. This removes empty AABB wedges without ignoring real walls or tiny obstacles. F8 colors: real body mesh pink, source-sized hull green, broadphase blue, proposed path amber. See [TRAINS.md](TRAINS.md) and [validation/v0311](validation/v0311) for measurements, behavior matrix and coverage limits.

## Geometry and coordinates

One horizontal tile =one block. An integer endpoint names a base cell; its centerline is `(x+.5,y+.125,z+.5)`. The visible ballast bed is2blocks wide, rails at±.72; ordinary cardinal paths touch a3-cell technical strip. This is **thin** physical geometry, not three solid cubes. It never replaces a neighboring solid block. Each occupied cell gets only the clipped local bed/rail/sleeper geometry and collision. End supports remain original blocks; minute eased-slope underside fragments are clipped at the two foundation cells.

Sixteen endpoint heading families: cardinal, diagonal and `(1:2)/(2:1)` half diagonals, with rotations/reflections. Horizontal cubic handles are selected deterministically and checked for forward monotonicity and minimum radius13. Arc length uses a1024-step deterministic table with interpolated inverse lookup. Height uses smoothstep with zero endpoint grade, maximum1:4 and128-block maximum arc length; vertical rise is limited24blocks. Tangent/side/up-normal and reverse samples are explicit future bogie APIs. No block-step teleporting or separate elevated-rail item/technology exists.

**Geometry adaptation:** these are variable Minecraft cubic connectors with Factorio2.x endpoint families/radius, not literal fixed-footprint Factorio A/B entity templates or Factorio1.1 curves. Curves/S-bends/slopes can use shapes between the original discrete templates. Source straight pricing is retained: two cardinal tiles /two diagonal tiles cost1rail, a `(2,4)` half-diagonal costs2. Variable curved/inclined sections cost `ceil(arcLength/2)`, so their price is a documented Minecraft adaptation rather than a claim that every source A/B piece costs its original3items.

## Topology, switches and crossings

A saved segment has a UUID and stable `Directed(UUID, reverse)` traversal identity. Node identity includes integer position and tangent axis, never just proximity. Only explicit compatible endpoints join. A turnout is one endpoint shared by incoming, straight and tangent-continuous branching curves; its outgoing alternatives are graph edges, not random minecart choices. Reverse traversal exposes the legitimate incoming route without an automatic U-turn. Up to8local segments share a physical cell.

Unrelated same-level crossings are **explicit placement conflicts** in this foundation. They do not create a hidden junction. A shared-endpoint turnout permits its local24-block conflict envelope; train spatial reservations treat intersecting swept turnout bounds as conflicting. Short straight approach edges within8blocks are allowed only through actual graph connectivity, so consecutive two-tile pieces do not collide with their own future carriage envelope. Unrelated nearby tracks receive no exemption. A geometric overpass with sufficient separation stays disconnected. At-grade diamonds/crossover primitives and movable switch blades are not implemented. Mid-edge splitting is not yet a player action: build a junction at a chosen segment endpoint; demolition/rejoin edits that edge without changing siblings.

## Placement and protection

Hold the single Rail item. Plain ground-use builds a real short piece: two cardinal/diagonal tiles for one rail, or one (2,4) half-diagonal for two rails. It passes ordinary supports, clearance, protection and actual inventory payment; it leaves no invisible selection. Click a placed physical rail to select its nearest compatible canonical endpoint/tangent, then click supported ground or another rail endpoint to connect. A curved body hit chooses a real endpoint; it never invents a mid-edge junction. **Success clears selection**, so further extension requires an explicit reselect. R cycles goal direction, X reverses start; **C or sneak-use cancels, including in air**. Slot change, spectator/death, world/logout, lost/removed/unavailable anchor and a 60-second inactivity bound revoke stale selections. Server binds the selection to the canonical segment UUID, dimension and selected hotbar slot.

Preview separates geometry from permission to build. Valid routes are green; blocked, too tight, steep, unloaded, out-of-reach or unaffordable routes retain a red ghost and localized reason. Blue endpoints, actual bed and amber future-clearance envelopes are distinct. MISS has no ghost. An active plan can inspect a real surface up to 64 blocks away to explain range failure; actual placement still uses the normal server block interaction distance. Geometry is cached by endpoints; local validity refresh is bounded to once per four ticks, plus selection/endpoint changes. A fresh selection packet establishes the current client level before the next tick, fixing the first-tick intermittent reset. Old previews vanished because geometry validation exceptions bypassed rendering; nonowning preview curves now survive those exceptions, while server plans/saved segments explicitly reject them.

Shared `RailPlan` checks every touched bed and future-clearance voxel, border/build height, loaded chunks, solid endpoint foundations and physical entity collision. Future full-sized carriage clearance is conservatively a3×7×4body sampled at quarter-block intervals, using horizontal tangent-aligned AABB sweeps; diagonal/curve clearance may exceed the nominal straight3-cell strip. Both endpoint supports are exempt from the tiny abutment clip, never replaced. Intermediate spans may be unsupported: structural bridge supports are player-built, not free generated foundations. Tunnels must be excavated first; placement never deletes terrain/fluids/plants.

Server additionally checks real research/material availability, adventure/build permissions and a NeoForge multi-place protection event over **all touched voxels**, including unchanged clearance. Ordinary subsequent block placement is also refused inside a reserved carriage envelope. World writes are staged; cancellation or changed inventory rolls back before charging. Materials are component-exact stacks, not just an item count. Cost is escrowed once into the saved segment after revalidation. Preview cannot predict another mod's server-only claims; the final authority can reject a green geometric preview.

Wrench or break targets one nearest segment in the clicked cell. Removal checks permissions across that segment and requires loaded touched chunks. The saved refund claim retires before modifying projections or handing materials back; repeat deletion returns nothing. Environmental replacement retires affected claims once; late orphan projections disappear when their chunk loads. Technical parts have no block item, independent loot or inventory.

## Persistence and rendering

`RailwayData` is dimension-local SavedData, schema1: endpoint/control definition, UUID and exactly one list of paid native component-bearing ItemStacks per segment. Runtime indexes map physical cells/chunks to edge IDs; graph edits touch incident endpoints. Tracks have no tickers or per-tick global traversal. Saved topology persists independently of loaded availability. Early chunk-unload events quarantine live membership; reload is idempotent and retains IDs. Availability additionally requires all swept chunks loaded. No world access occurs on worker threads.

`RailCellBlockEntity` stores identity-only membership, compact nonowning edge descriptors, local collision and immutable mesh projection. It never stores Paid/Items/FE. Projection versions may rebuild rendering/collision from the canonical saved curve without creating resources. Native tracking BlockEntity packets synchronize changes/initial chunks; no all-world graph broadcast. Client keeps bounded curve projections and weak per-cell baked-quad cache. Models render in ordinary compiled chunks, not through per-rail entities/BERs or per-frame topology searches.

`/factorycraft rail` reports edge/node counts, revision, loaded availability and actual owned rail-item totals. Empty-hand right-click reports segment ID, metric length, endpoint degree and availability. Current APIs are ready for later station edge points, directed path search, switch reservations and bogie sampling; none of those later game systems is simulated here.
